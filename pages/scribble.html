Scribble is designed for the toddler years — when everything is new and nothing stays the same for long. Crayon colours, playful doodle motifs, and a layout that balances milestone moments with the beautiful everyday chaos.

Printed on 170gsm FSC-certified matte art paper. Hardcover case-bound. Produced in Vienna, dispatched within 5–7 business days.

  • Format: 21 × 21 cm (square)
  • Cover: Matte linen hardcover, warm grey
  • Paper: 170gsm matte, archival quality
  • Binding: Case-bound, sewn signatures

A 40-page book uses 35–50 photos; 80 pages uses 70–90. JPEG or RAW both work.

  • Minimum resolution: 2000px on the short edge
  • Colour space: sRGB preferred
  • We accept Google Drive, Dropbox, or direct upload
  • Austria: 2–3 business days
  • Germany, Switzerland: 3–4 business days
  • Rest of EU: 5–7 business days
