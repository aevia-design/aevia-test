Newborn is designed for the first days — a quiet, considered structure built around the birth story and the tiny details worth keeping. The layout balances full-page imagery with typeset captions, giving each spread room to breathe.

We handle all design and typesetting. You supply the photos and a few key details, and our team composes the final layout for your approval before printing.

Printed on 170gsm FSC-certified matte art paper. Hardcover case-bound with linen-texture finish. Produced in Vienna, dispatched within 5–7 business days of approval.

  • Format: 21 × 21 cm (square)
  • Cover: Matte linen hardcover, warm grey
  • Paper: 170gsm matte, archival quality
  • Binding: Case-bound, sewn signatures

After ordering you'll receive a simple upload link. A 40-page book uses 35–50 photos; 80 pages uses 70–90. JPEG or RAW both work. We'll flag anything that won't print well before starting.

  • Minimum resolution: 2000px on the short edge
  • Colour space: sRGB preferred
  • We accept Google Drive, Dropbox, or direct upload

Delivery estimates from approval date:

  • Austria: 2–3 business days
  • Germany, Switzerland: 3–4 business days
  • Rest of EU: 5–7 business days
  • UK, USA: 7–10 business days

Standard shipping included. Express available at checkout.
