Tender is built around your wedding day. Generous margins and a delicate hand-script accent give every image the space to speak. Its story pages hold the words a photo can't: an opening note, how the two of you began, and your vows beside a full-bleed photo.

Printed on 170gsm FSC-certified matte art paper. Hardcover case-bound with linen-texture finish. Designed in Vienna · Printed in the EU.

  • Format: 20 × 20 cm (square)
  • Cover: Matte linen hardcover, ivory
  • Paper: 170gsm matte, archival quality
  • Binding: Case-bound, sewn signatures

After ordering you'll receive a simple upload link. JPEG or RAW both work. We'll tell you the ideal photo range when you order. We'll flag anything that won't print well before starting.

  • Minimum resolution: 2000px on the short edge
  • Colour space: sRGB preferred
  • We accept Google Drive, Dropbox, or direct upload
