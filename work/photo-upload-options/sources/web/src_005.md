# Artifact Uprising Help Center - Uploading Images / Uploading Photos with Google Photos

Source: https://help.artifactuprising.com/hc/en-us/articles/360000290332-Uploading-Images and https://help.artifactuprising.com/hc/en-us/articles/360002362832-Uploading-Photos-with-Google-Photos

## Extracted content
Artifact Uprising (the founding design inspiration for Aevia's product page, per project docs) accepts JPG, PNG, WEBP, and HEIC files up to 40MB each - the same 40MB ceiling Aevia uses. PDF is not supported.

Upload sources supported: direct device upload, Google Photos (connect account in the editor), and mobile upload via QR code (phone-to-desktop handoff).
Explicitly not supported: Instagram cannot be imported directly "due to platform limitations." No mention of Dropbox, Google Drive, or iCloud as supported sources; the help center directs customers with images on unsupported platforms to download to device first, then upload via the gallery.
