# Chatbooks Help Center - Why has adding Google Photos Changed?

Source: https://help.chatbooks.com/en/articles/10760721-why-has-adding-google-photos-changed
Accessed: 2026-09-24 (WebFetch extract)

## Extracted content
As of the article's most recent revision (referencing a July 2025-era interface change), Google Photos integration now redirects users out of the Chatbooks app/site into a browser for authentication, with new terms to accept.

Key friction points documented:
- Google Photos limits each connected session to one hour, with no in-app warning before it expires; uploads/selections in progress fail silently when the session times out.
- Chatbooks apps cap uploads at a maximum of 60 photos per Monthbook (min 30); anything beyond 60 is placed in an "excluded" section.
- Videos cannot be uploaded via this integration.
- Google Photos does not remember previously selected photos across sessions - users must reselect from scratch each time.
- Chatbooks' own recommended workaround: pre-build a dedicated Google Photos album before starting, to speed selection within the one-hour window.

## Notes
This is strong, specific, primary evidence of real UX friction inherent to cloud-source picker integrations even when they technically work - directly useful for research question 4 (does offering cloud sources reduce friction, or introduce new friction of its own). Session timeouts and re-selection burden are exactly the kind of cost a small team should weigh against the value of offering the integration.
