# Chatbooks Help Center - Instagram as a Photo Source

Source: https://help.chatbooks.com/en/articles/10139802-instagram-as-a-photo-source
Accessed: 2026-09-24 (WebSearch snippet aggregation)

## Extracted content
"Due to a recent change by Meta, all third-party connections to their Instagram API were removed late last year [2024], impacting Chatbooks." As a partial workaround, on iOS Chatbooks added a "Quick Add" flow that goes the opposite direction - sharing photos FROM Chatbooks TO Instagram - rather than importing from Instagram.
Separately, Chatbooks' general photo-source article states it supports Facebook, Google Photos, Dropbox, and Flickr as import sources, plus device camera roll and app upload, and additionally lists SmugMug and HeyFam.

## Notes
Direct, named, dated (per Meta's Dec 2024 Instagram Basic Display API shutdown, corroborated independently in src_012) confirmation that a competitor's Instagram photo-import integration was killed by a platform change outside their control - not a business decision. Relevant to Aevia's question about which integrations are durable vs. fragile.
