# Threadgold Consulting - Personal Cloud Storage Usage: How Many People Use The Cloud? (2025)

Source: https://threadgoldconsulting.com/research/personal-cloud-storage-usage
Accessed: 2026-09-24 (WebSearch snippet aggregation)

## Extracted content
Personal cloud storage users grew from an estimated 1.1 billion in 2014 to roughly 2.3 billion in 2025. Photo storage is cited as a dominant use case, driven by smartphone cameras and auto-backup features. 55% of cloud users are reported to rely on at least three different cloud services simultaneously (e.g., photos in Google Photos, documents in Dropbox, iPhone backups in iCloud) - i.e., different services for different content TYPES, not interchangeable general-purpose photo stores. Of the roughly 50% of people who store photos in the cloud, 43% cite backup as their primary reason (vs. sharing, cross-device viewing, or editing).

## Notes
Market-research/consultancy source rather than a primary survey with disclosed methodology - moderate authority, treat figures as directional rather than precise. Still useful for research question 2: the "3+ services, one per content type" pattern supports the idea that Google Photos and iCloud Photos (the two photo-specific stores) are the realistic cloud sources for a customer's actual photo library, while Dropbox/Drive are more likely to hold documents or work files than a phone's everyday photo backup.
