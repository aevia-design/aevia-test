## Extracted content
Nearly three-quarters of iPhone owners have never transferred or backed up their photos to a computer or external drive, relying solely on iCloud or on-device storage. Typical iPhone user takes ~62 photos/week (~9/day), roughly 3,200 new photos per year. An estimated 1.94 trillion photos were projected to be taken in 2024 across all devices.

## Notes
Aggregator/statistics-roundup site rather than a primary research body - moderate-to-lower authority, and the "121 statistics" format suggests figures are compiled from many original sources of varying quality, so treat headline numbers as indicative rather than exact. Still relevant to research question 2: it reinforces that for iPhone users specifically (a large share of Aevia's premium customer base), iCloud Photos (not Google Drive or Dropbox) is the default, often-untouched home for their photo library - supporting the case that if any single cloud source integration were worth adding, it would need to reach iCloud/Google Photos, not Drive/Dropbox.
