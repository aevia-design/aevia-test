So we built the opposite of a blank screen: a small studio in Vienna, a few templates, each designed with artists and illustrators we work with directly. You choose the one that fits your occasion, send your photos, and we compose the book by hand.

Fewer choices. A book worth keeping.

Option J

The rolling row

One row, four photos at different heights on a shared centre line, drifting slowly left. Edges fade so photos arrive and leave rather than pop. Hover anywhere to pause it.

The studio, most days.

Option K

Rolling, slower and bigger

Same idea, larger photos and a slower drift — fewer on screen at once, so each one is properly readable as it passes.

Option L

The same row, not moving

Identical composition with no animation — it simply runs off the right edge and you scroll or swipe it. Shown so you can judge whether the motion is adding anything.
