## Product pages (short, coded path segments — a contrast case)

- Hardcover photo books: `/p/pb/hpb/`
- Softcover photo books: `/p/pb/spb/`

## Note
Unlike Artifact Uprising, Mixbook and Saal Digital (descriptive slugs), Journi uses short cryptic codes (`p`, `pb`, `hpb`) rather than descriptive words — a counter-example against the "always use descriptive words" guidance from Google/NNG.
