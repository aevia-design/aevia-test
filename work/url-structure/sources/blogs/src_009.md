# Subdomains vs Subfolders (Subdirectories) for SEO

Source: https://ahrefs.com/blog/subdomain-vs-subfolder/
Publisher: Ahrefs (SEO tools/education blog)
Retrieved: 2026-09-23 via WebFetch (summarized extract)

## Authority Consolidation

Common misconception debunked: subdomains aren't automatically treated as separate domains. Both subdomains and subdirectories consolidate authority with the main site if internally linked and presented as part of the same website.

## Case Study Findings

Real migrations (mention.com, GitHub) show no meaningful SEO advantage for one structure over the other. Traffic shifts resulted from other factors — additional content, internal linking changes, redesigns — not the URL structure itself.

## Official Google Position Cited

- Matt Cutts (2012): "They're roughly equivalent. I would basically go with whichever one is easier for you in terms of configuration."
- John Mueller (2017): "Google websearch is fine with using either subdomains or subdirectories."

## Recommendation

Choose based on technical convenience, not SEO theory. Avoid migrating solely for SEO reasons — restructuring introduces unnecessary risk. Focus on internal linking and content strategy instead.
