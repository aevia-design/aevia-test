## What was diagnosed (design-review @1440px)
Original newborn product page had three measurable issues:
1. **Gallery too narrow** — ~40% of viewport vs competitors' ~50–60% (panel 400px + gap 72px inside 1160px container).
2. **Dead space below gallery** — gallery 500px vs panel 815px = 315px blank on the left while the panel still had content.
3. **Accordion body too narrow** — `.acc-body{max-width:600px}` used only 52% of the 1160px width.

Competitor check: Artifact Uprising = ~50/50 split, 8 thumbnails in a single horizontal row below the hero; Papier = full-width below-the-fold accordions.

## Changes applied to `pages/newborn.html` (REAL page, uncommitted)
- `.zone`: `max-width 1160→1280px`, `grid-template-columns: 1fr 360px` (was 400px), `gap 72→56px` → wider gallery (~40%→~50%).
- Hero `.gallery-main`: `aspect-ratio 10/7 → 5/4` (taller).
- Thumbnails: 4 → **6** in a **3×2 grid** (`aspect-ratio 3/2`); added `fpintro` + `fplabour` as thumbs 5–6.
- **Prev/next arrow buttons** overlaid on the hero (`.gallery-arrow`); `navArrow()` cycles + syncs the active thumb AND the lightbox image when open.
- **Keyboard ← / →** navigation (extended the existing Escape `keydown` listener), works on the page and inside the lightbox.
- `.acc-body` `max-width 600→800px`.
- Copy fix: Labour spread desc → "A welcome line and, optionally, your baby's zodiac." (zodiac is optional; stop-slopped).

## Owner's conclusions (carry forward)
1. **Newborn layout is liked** (the `-exp` direction: taller hero, small single-row thumbs). Treat as the reference.
2. **Scribble is NOT yet ideal.** Real tension: templates with many special pages have a tall panel, so either (A) make thumbnails a bit bigger to fill the dead space, or (B) keep them small (current scribble-exp) and accept that even 2 rows leave a lot of dead space. **Trade-off unresolved.** Owner accepts "some dead space is OK — better than inconsistent thumb sizes across templates."
3. **Blocked on mockups.** Owner wants to **re-do the closed-book and back-cover mockup views** (doesn't like them) before finalising. The whole gallery exercise should be **re-tested once new mockups exist** — bigger-vs-smaller-thumbnail decision is easier to judge with imagery worth showing.

## Standard that emerged (proposed, not locked)
Consistent gallery presentation across all templates beats matching each panel's height: same thumb size, same grid logic, hero + small thumbs (single row when few images, 2 centred rows when more), one special-page example rather than the full catalogue. Dead space is a predictable side effect of fewer special pages and is acceptable.
