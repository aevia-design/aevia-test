### 16:10 — Every upload had been failing since S154's push
**Done:** One line moved in `pages/order.html` (`61c22a4`). `const workerInFlightBytes` was
declared at 2470 but first read at 2437 via `updateProgress()`, called at 2455.
**Why:** Reading a `const` before its declaration throws — the temporal dead zone. The
`|| {}` fallback on the read never ran, because touching the name IS the error. It would
have worked with `var`, which is why the guard looked sufficient to everyone who read it.
**Watch out for:** The owner hit this on a 3G test and assumed throttling. It fails on ANY
connection — `updateProgress()` runs before a single byte moves. **Ordering was broken on
the live rig from `10536f2` until this fix.** Verified after: his 3G upload completed.

### 18:40 — Front cover captions ignored the widened spine (all templates)
**Done:** `coverCaptionShiftMm()` in `scripts/export-pdf.js` + `tests/cover-caption-shift.test.js`
(`27ffeeb`). Test written first, confirmed red, then fixed. 312 tests pass.
**Why:** `renderCoverImage` shifts front-panel photo slots right by `spineWidth −
referenceSpine`, and both engines shift front captions by the same delta (`capDeltaPx`).
`drawCoverCaptions` did not. Newborn exposed it because its name caption and photo share a
centre (`xMm: 327`): on screen "Nico" sat under the photo's middle, in print 5mm left of it.
**Watch out for:** **Every template was affected** — all five declare `referenceSpineMm: 9`,
so 40pp was already 1mm out and 80pp is 5mm. Scribble and Tender were signed off as "proven
in print" in S153 carrying this; 1mm went unnoticed because their captions are not visually
tied to a photo centre. `customer-preview` already had the correct logic — only the PDF was wrong.
**Requires a Cloud Run redeploy** — the renderer `require`s `scripts/export-pdf.js` and
carries its own frozen copy. Owner redeployed and regenerated.
