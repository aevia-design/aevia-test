### 15:40 — #112 opened, diagnosed wrongly, then fixed
**Done:** A failed photo used to `throw` out of its worker's `while` loop, so that worker
stopped pulling work permanently. The pool bled 5→0 and the queue was never finished
(AEV-096: slot 29 failed, slots 30–56 never attempted). Now recorded and the worker continues;
the error is raised once after all five settle. Commit `bb311f1`.
**Decided:** Consecutive-failure breaker, **limit 5, reset to zero on any success**
(`work/upload-worker-resilience/decision.md`). Five equals `CONCURRENCY`, so tripping it means
every worker in flight failed with no success between. Rejected: no breaker (~9 min to report
on a dead link), total-failure limit, wall-clock limit (the mistake S151 undid).
**Why:** The first write-up blamed `Promise.all` and was **corrected** (`b6d320d`) — the
workers keep running; the real defect is the worker dying.
**Watch out for:** **The first test passed on the broken code.** Failing one slot leaves four
workers to drain the queue. Reproducing it needs **five scattered failures**. Pre-fix now
reports "35 slots never attempted"; post-fix "all 51 healthy photos uploaded".
