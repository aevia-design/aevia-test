## What happened

### 1. Diagnosed the post-trial bill
- Owner set a $10/month budget, hit a 50% alert within days of the trial ending. Worried testing was uneconomical.
- Investigated with `gcloud`/`gsutil` (project `aevia-uploads`). Note: gcloud trips over the Windows Python alias — set `CLOUDSDK_PYTHON` to the bundled interpreter (`…\google-cloud-sdk\platform\bundledpython\python.exe`).
- Bucket `aevia-uploads.firebasestorage.app` = 6.83 GiB across 11 orders (AEV-031…041) after the owner trimmed old orders. **Storage is ~€0.14/mo — irrelevant.**
- Owner's own billing chatbot + Reports: **99.7% of cost was egress** ("Download Worldwide Destinations", SKU `22EB-AAE8-FBCD`). Root cause: full-res originals (~6 MB/photo, 1–2 GB/order) re-downloaded on every engine view and every local PDF run; heavy template testing = ~55 GB egress.
- **Verified the real unit rate** from the owner's post-trial figures: **€5.62 / 54.53 GiB = €0.103/GB** (do NOT divide whole-period €8.62 / 183.66 GiB — most of those GiB were free trial usage; that gives a misleading €0.047).

### 2. Decision (via /ideating → /solutioning → ADR-0005)
Options weighed: #1 web-res previews, #3 CDN, #6 server-side in-region PDF, #7 R2 migration.
- **Commit #1 + #6; defer #3; park #7.** Reversibility framing: #1/#6/#3 are two-way doors, #7 is one-way (vendor migration). #1 is the real fix (10–25× on screen loads, zero on-screen quality loss — 1600px is pixel-identical to a 24 MP original on screen). #6 needed for prod ops anyway + makes the PDF read internal. #3 second-order after #1. #7 YAGNI after #1+#6.
- Recorded in `docs/decisions/0005-egress-cost-web-res-previews-server-side-pdf.md`.
