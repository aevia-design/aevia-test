## 2. Open-spread BLANK STRIP on the fore-edge — FIXED (composer-only)
Evgeny flagged: right page looked shoved into the fold, with a blank near-white band on the right
fore-edge. Measured the actual pixels (didn't theorise):
- Every spread capture is 3780px wide but the real two-page content is only `[0, 3611]` — there is
  **~168px of `.spread-pages` container background (#fafafa ≈ 250,250,248) captured on the RIGHT
  edge only** (0 on the left). Left page = pure white 255; right container = 250,250,248 (distinct).
- So splitting the raw frame at width/2 (1890) landed ~80px RIGHT of the true gutter (~1805):
  the right page's inner strip went into the fold AND the right slot inherited the container strip
  as the blank fore-edge band. NOT a "pages behind" visualisation — just captured background.
- **FIX** in `compose-mockup.mjs`: detect the real content box (trim uniform container-bg margins
  on all 4 edges via `isContainer`/`colContent`/`rowContent`) and split THAT in half, not the raw
  frame. Composer-only, works on the EXISTING captures (no re-capture / password needed).
- **Bonus:** same root cause fixed S61's diagnosed-but-unfixed `open-11-fp1` map-spread issue
  (right-page blank + gutter "connection area") for free. No separate decision needed.

## 3. Open-spread CORNER OVERSHOOT (art spills past the page onto the cover) — FIXED
Evgeny flagged: on yellow/content LEFT pages the art ran past the page edge at the top-left and
bottom-left corners. Cause: the PSD page silhouettes are CURVED (open-book perspective — `Page left`
is y≈482–1497 at the outer edge but y≈469–1510 mid-page), while the art slots are axis-aligned
RECTANGLES from each layer's bounding box (y 466–1514). So the rectangle's outer corners poke past
the real page edge and the multiply art darkens the cover there. (Right page same, but hidden under
the cover-edge tint — that's why only the left was visible.)
- **FIX** in `compose-mockup.mjs`: `half()` now clips each art half's ALPHA by an `alphaSampler` =
  union of the white page base (`Page left`/`Page right`, the curved silhouette) and the multiply
  layer (`Left page `/`Right page`, which reaches the fold). Corners trimmed, gutter still fills.
  Art halves are now raw RGBA (was PNG) so the baked alpha clips correctly on the `multiply`
  composite. Applied to BOTH pages.
