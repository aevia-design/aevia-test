### 09:15 — Full-bleed photos ignored the reposition offset in print
**Done:** `export-pdf.js` full-bleed branch now calls the shared `coverExtract` with
`state.heartCrop` instead of hardcoding `position:'centre'`. `tests/photo-crop-paths.test.js`
guards all three photo branches.
**Why:** owner repositioned `FPhim` in the Heirloom orders and it printed centred. The
exporter has three photo paths — heart, regular, full-bleed — and #74 (S50) taught only the
first two to read the offset. The full-bleed branch has been centring since it was written
on 2026-05-26.
**Watch out for:** **this is not Heirloom-only.** Every template has a full-bleed slot
(Heirloom `FPhim`/`FPher`, Joyride `SP3`/`SP6`, Scribble `FP2`, Papercut `FP2`, Tender
`FPwords`); Scribble's and Papercut's are `pool:'regular'`, so ordinary customer photos are
in scope. **Whether any real order ever misprinted is UNPROVEN** — owner pushed back saying
his past full-bleed prints looked right, and he is probably correct: on a square slot only
ONE axis overflows, so a sideways drag on a portrait photo moves nothing in any of the three
surfaces and they all agree. Measured: portrait 2000×3127 into the 206mm square gives
overflowX **0px**, overflowY 1371px. Real-print check deferred to the Printsmarter samples
run as TO-DOS #102. Also: the fix is **not** byte-identical for un-repositioned photos —
`coverExtract` resizes-then-extracts where `fit:'cover'` does one pass, measured max 2/255 on
0.7% of pixels. The pre-existing #74 comment claiming byte-identity is therefore wrong too.

### 10:20 — Customer preview crashed on every template, one day old
**Root cause:** `renderCover` threw `ReferenceError: color is not defined`. Introduced the
previous morning in `e1efd85` (S160) by the light-ink caption treatment for the dark
Brown/Green covers — the line was ported from `template-engine.html`, which computes `color`
inline in that scope, but customer-preview had already moved that computation into
`applyCoverCaptionLayout`. Fixed by reading `cap.style.color` back after layout, the idiom
the file already uses for spread captions.
**Watch out for:** **the failure was partial, which is what made it dangerous** — the book
failed to render while Approve and Pay stayed live, so a customer could approve a blank book.
It hit all six templates, not just Heirloom. **`qa/heirloom-preview-mock.mjs` already covered
it** (verified: 0/1 on the buggy file, 8/8 on the fixed one) — it was simply never re-run
after S160 touched customer-preview. A process gap, not a coverage gap: `npm test` does not
execute the preview, same shape as the S154/S156 `order.html` lesson.

### 12:10 — 80pp test photo set
**Done:** `assets/test photos/Wedding-112/` — the 56 Wedding photos renamed `001`–`056` with
duplicates as `057`–`112`. Gitignored, 432 MB.
**Decided:** new folder rather than renaming in place, and **zero-padded**. Padding makes the
picker's lexicographic sort match the numbering, which puts every photo exactly **56
positions** from its duplicate — the maximum separation, so no repeat can share a spread.
Plain `1,2,3` sorts as 1,10,100,… and scatters pairs unpredictably.
**Watch out for:** `Wedding/`'s descriptive names are deliberately preserved — LEARNINGS
(S158) calls it the subject-matched set, and `001.png` tells you nothing about what it shows.
