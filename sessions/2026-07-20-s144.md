## What happened

Started as "guidance and questions about the migration plan", became the migration.

**Three questions answered first (Q1–Q3):**
- **Stock photos vs none, for indexing:** images play no meaningful role in indexing; stock buys nothing and carries brand + duplicate-content risk. Ship with none.
- **Is migrating now safe for future indexing:** yes, and it is the safer choice — migrating before Google indexes anything avoids a future 301 chain and ranking loss.
- **Does the plan need changes:** yes, the DE site (built S141, after the brief was written) added hreflang as new work and doubled the canonical/robots/banner work. Folded into the brief.

**Then a `/solutioning` pass on Q2 changed the plan.** The brief had the live site indexed from day one. The decisive insight: `noindex` and "don't crawl" are different levers — a `noindex` *header* still lets Google crawl and discover every URL. So indexing now preserved nothing that noindex loses, while costing a draft-copy first impression. Decision: **live site stays noindex until launch**, via a Cloudflare header rule.

## Phases

**Phase 2 — gate + waitlist.** `pages/waitlist.html` with an embedded Brevo form; `assets/js/site-mode.js` (host-aware noindex + launch banner + order-gate belt); include added to 25 EN+DE pages. The Brevo form took four rounds to collect anything — the form was configured with double opt-in but no sender existed at `aevia.at` (only the owner's personal Gmail was verified). Endpoint returned `{"success":true}` throughout, which is how it hid.

**Phase 3 — the cutover.** The careful hour, owner-driven:
1. Registrar export → **14 records**, not the 9 the earlier probe found (see Learnings).
2. Cloudflare zone created; auto-scan imported 10 and set three mail CNAMEs to **Proxied**, which would have broken DKIM. Fixed to DNS-only.
3. Exports diffed programmatically — all 14 identical, character for character.
4. Three rules created; nameservers switched at helloly; ~30 min to propagate.
5. **Mail verified before the site went public.** Brevo→Yahoo `dkim=pass (s=brevo2) spf=pass dmarc=pass`; Xenia M365→Gmail pass; inbound + Firebase reset delivered.
6. Pages custom domains attached → site live, all three rules active.

**Phase 4 — SEO.** `robots.txt` (overrides Cloudflare's injected Content Signals Policy), canonical on 24 pages, hreflang on 11 EN/DE pairs. Homepage-at-root **deferred** — a 200 rewrite 404s the bare-filename nav links, and `<base>` breaks the fragment links.

**Phase 5 — app follows the domain.** `siteOrigin(req)` replaces four hardcoded literals; logo off the Webflow CDN; `location.origin` in the dashboard; CORS anchored. Docs + both ADRs corrected.

**Wander CSV sync.** Owner edited the sizing CSV in parallel: SP0 background, SP0/SP3 caption Y, and a new `overlay_position=below` on SP5 → `overlayAbovePhotos:false`. All 36 standard slots verified against the CSV.

## Corrections to the brief (recorded in ADR-0009)

1. **The record table was incomplete.** 9 found by probe, 14 in the registrar export. The 5 extras were Firebase auth-email records.
2. **Phase order was impossible.** Pages **apex** custom domains require the zone to already be active, so nameservers must move first. The corrected order is also safer: mail is verified while the site is still invisible.
3. **Cloudflare Rules only apply to proxied hostnames** — the gate stays inert until Pages makes the hostname orange. No gap results, but the rules look broken if tested early.
