### — Caption multi-line fixed (innerText vs textContent)
**Done:** Switched caption save/restore from `textContent` to `innerText` in all three places (initial render, focus handler, blur handler).
**Why:** `textContent` strips `<br>` elements that Shift+Enter inserts in contenteditable — line breaks were lost on every blur. `innerText` round-trips `\n` ↔ `<br>` correctly.
**Watch out for:** Shift+Enter creates a new line; plain Enter still saves and closes (by design). Do not revert to `textContent` for caption elements.

### — PDF export architecture DECIDED (/solutioning)
**Decided:** Server-side **Sharp + pdf-lib** compositing, built as a **standalone Node.js tool first**, then wired to order path. Rejected Puppeteer/Playwright `page.pdf()`.
**Why:** Browser PDF pipeline embeds raster images at rendered resolution (CSS px × devicePixelRatio), NOT photo native resolution — quality is capped by the 600px preview canvas regardless of deviceScaleFactor, and the behaviour is undocumented/version-dependent. Print needs a separate pipeline that composites ORIGINAL photos at 300dpi. Sharp gives deterministic pixel control and maps cleanly to the future GCS flow.
**Watch out for:** Do NOT revisit browser-based PDF for the production print path. Print specs (3mm content bleed, 18mm cover bleed, bleed = page bgColor not white, single pages not spreads) are in `LEARNINGS.md`. Roadmap renumbered: Plan 11 = standalone PDF export (11-01 export state JSON, 11-02 content pages quality gate, 11-03 captions, 11-04 cover), Plan 12 = order flow integration (was Plan 11).
