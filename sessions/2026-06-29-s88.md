## Decisions / research

- **Password best practices** (`/conducting-research`, NIST 800-63B Rev 4 2025 + Firebase docs): no composition rules, length over complexity, allow paste/password managers, offer show-password. Aevia choice = **min 8, no composition, ENFORCE** (NIST's 15-char ideal judged too heavy for premium-consumer checkout; Google sign-in covers the no-password path).
- **Email-verification gate is intentional**: a password account's email is NOT verified by Firebase on signup. Requiring `email_verified` is the privacy spine of the ownership model. Google sign-ins are verified automatically. (This is why the staff account `evg.myasin@gmail.com` — created via password in chunk-018 — hits the verify screen on account.html.)
- **Ownership continuity confirmed working**: Evgeny's pre-feature guest orders (placed with his personal email before accounts existed) surfaced automatically after sign-in — email-match, no schema migration.

## Watch-outs (S88)

- **Token-staleness gotcha (fixed):** right after a user verifies their email, the cached Firebase ID token still carries `email_verified:false` until refreshed → `getMyOrders` 403s with "Please verify your email…". Fix: `account.html` `loadOrders` calls **`getIdToken(true)`** (force-refresh). If it ever recurs, sign-out/in mints a fresh token.
- **Backend-first held:** `getMyOrders` was deployed before the page reached `main`. If the function is ever re-deployed/removed, the live account page breaks.
- **Old orders with non-normalised email** (pre-order-flow-hardening, mixed-case) may not match (Firestore `==` is case-sensitive). Accepted Phase-1 limitation.
- **Google sign-in for an email that already has a password account** (e.g. evg.myasin@gmail.com) may hit `auth/account-exists-with-different-credential` under Firebase's default "one account per email". Not a bug; surfaced as a test caveat.
