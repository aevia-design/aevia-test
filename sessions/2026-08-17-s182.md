### 22:50 — firebase deploy fails on this machine; now in LEARNINGS
**Decided:** Recorded the `FUNCTIONS_DISCOVERY_TIMEOUT=120` fix in `LEARNINGS.md` (`d28b359`).
**Why:** `Cannot determine backend specification. Timeout after 10000` **names your code but is
