## Bug #1 — spine sub-label rendered tiny (real, fixed)
Owner typed "Summer, 2026" in the spine **sub**-label and it shrank to a fraction of the spine **title**'s size, even though both read 14pt in the editor.

**Root cause:** the spine sub box was only **20mm long along the spine** (`spineSub.wMm: 20`). "Summer, 2026" doesn't fit on one line at 14pt in 20mm, so it **wrapped to two lines** — and cover-caption `autoShrink` (which measures `scrollHeight` against the box's 5mm-thin band `hMm`) crushed the font down to force two lines into the band. The title sits in a 60mm box, fits on one line, barely shrinks — hence the mismatch.

**Fix:** owner widened the canonical `Joyride_Template_Sizing_Cover.csv` spine-sub to **40mm**; `joyride-data.js` still read the old `20`. Synced `spineSub.wMm 20 → 40` ([joyride-data.js:83](../assets/Template_Joyride/joyride-data.js#L83)). Now the date fits on one line; both spine labels shrink the same ~1pt for the 5mm band, so they read matched. (On the rotated spine, `wMm` is the *visual height* running along the spine — that's why the owner called it "height".)
