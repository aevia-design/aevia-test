### 15:10 — Three data-driven mechanics, not three Heirloom special cases
**Done:** Mandatory spreads, grouped add-ons and the monogram, all in `order.html`.
**Decided:** Each keys off a template-data property (`mandatory`, `addonGroup`,
`monograms`) rather than a template name check, so all three are genuine no-ops
elsewhere — verified: `heirloom-data.js` is the ONLY file declaring any of them.
**Why:** `addonGroup` was already declared in the data and read NOWHERE, so buying
"Why I love him & her" would have produced a section with no fields and two empty
spreads in the book. Found by the owner testing the deployed form, not by any test.
**Watch out for:** URL-supplied slugs keep their case (`FPstory`); slugs the form
generates itself (mandatory, group members) are lowercased. `fpKeyForSlug` resolves
either, so don't sprinkle `toLowerCase()` at call sites — normalise at the source if
it ever bites.

### 15:20 — Monogram travels in fpTexts, chosen on the product page
**Decided:** `fpTexts.monogram` + `fpTexts.monogramLetters`, the same free ride
`zodiacSign` takes. NO order-form picker — the product page owns the choice (owner).
**Why:** `functions/upload.js` and `saveStaffState` both whitelist their fields;
`fpTexts` passes through verbatim, so this needs no backend change and no deploy.
The owner's call on the picker: choosing between three names with no artwork visible

**Watch out for:** an order URL without `&monogram=` silently gets `defaultMonogram`

appends it — revisit at Stage 8. `services/pdf-renderer/index.js` carries it into the
render state and **ships in the Cloud Run image**: without a redeploy every Heirloom
book prints Roots regardless of what was ordered.

### 15:45 — Monogram letters: position is right, letterforms are not
**Decided:** Do NOT nudge coordinates to fix the lopsided look. Xenia to widen the
artwork pockets; 18pt size drop is the interim if needed.
**Why:** measured — boxes sit exactly where the CSV says (Roots 0px offset, Birds
5mm, Roses 29mm). The 8mm letter box is the problem: `M` at 23pt inks **7.62mm**,
`A` only 5.47mm. Swap test decisive — the crowding follows the M, not the position.
**Watch out for:** **box WIDTH has no visual effect** — a centred single glyph
depends only on the box CENTRE (the width cancels algebraically in the PDF, and both
engines use `overflow:visible`). Telling Xenia to change `captions_Width_mm` would
change nothing. The real levers are `caption_size_pt` and the artwork itself.
