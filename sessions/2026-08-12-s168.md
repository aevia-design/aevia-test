### The drop could not have shipped as delivered

`assets/Template_Laguna/` arrived with the artwork embedded as base64 RGBA PNGs. The cover was
**36 MB** and every continent map 7–11 MB. Two hard thresholds sit above those numbers: >25 MiB
silently fails the Cloudflare deploy and takes the whole site stale, and >8 MB silently drops
the SVG from the print PDF. So the cover could not be deployed and no map could be printed.

Worse, the cover and the Africa map **could not be parsed by librsvg at all** — the base64 blob
exceeded libxml's 10 MB per-line buffer (`XML_PARSE_HUGE`). They were closer to broken than to
merely large.

`scripts/optimise-laguna-rasters.mjs` (new, re-runnable) decodes each raster, downsamples to
**300 DPI**, flattens the alpha onto the flat colour behind it, and re-encodes as JPEG q92 with
4:4:4 chroma. **85 MB → 11.7 MB**; the cover 34.8 → 3.5 MB.

Verified rather than assumed: pixel-diffed every original against its replacement, **mean
difference 0.6–0.7 / 255**, 99.9% of pixels within 3%. Clémence's cover was **406 DPI**, so
300 discards only what the press discards anyway.

⚠ **The original 85 MB drop is not in git and not recoverable from this repo.** The owner needs
to archive the master SVGs externally.

### I got the spine wrong, and it mattered

I claimed the six older templates (`referenceSpineMm: 9`) were "slightly wrong in print" and
needed a remediation session. **Wrong** — and wrong in a direction that would have sent six
correct covers back to Xenia for re-export.

The owner set it straight. The print spec is fixed for every template: **40pp → 10mm, 80pp →
14mm**, returned by `getSpineWidthMm(pageCount)` on all three surfaces. `referenceSpineMm`
records only **what the artwork was drawn against**; the code shifts by
`delta = getSpineWidthMm − reference`. Wander at 9 gets +1 at 40pp; Laguna at 10 gets 0 at 40pp
and **+4 at 80pp** — the owner's "+4mm for 80p", already handled by existing code.

The mistake was reading a field name as a statement about the **output** when it describes the
**input**. Recorded in `LEARNINGS.md`, in `docs/briefs/laguna-build.md` as an explicit
retraction, and as a new "Spine geometry — settled, do not re-litigate" section in the
`add-template` skill.

### "The cover photo isn't loading" — it was underneath the artwork

The owner's first eyeball found a blank white square where the cover photo should be. The photo
was fine: `blob:` src, `naturalWidth` 2000, correctly positioned. Clémence's SVG draws an
**opaque white `Frame` rect** at the photo position — the white margin *around* the photo, not
a window — and the artwork renders at `z-index: 2` against the slot's `1`.

A screenshot cannot tell a missing photo from an occluded one, and the smoke test's
`coverPhotosPlaced` counted the element as present either way. **Reading the live DOM stacking
order settled it in one query.**

Fix was pure data — `cover.overlayAbovePhotos: false` — because the mechanism already existed on
all three surfaces for Papercut. Same for the 26 interior page variants (`overlayBelow: true`,
from the owner's updated `overlay_position` CSV column). **Zero engine changes for either.**

### Two false alarms I raised and then disproved

- **`photosDecoded` reads 1 of 15** in the render smoke test. Not a Laguna defect: the engine
  emits `img.slot-photo` with an **empty `src`** and fills the blob URL elsewhere. **Wander
  scores identically (1 of 14)** through the same path. My first explanation (lazy loading) was
  wrong — scrolling changed nothing; probing the DOM gave the real answer.
- **`FP1/FP3/FP5/FPlabour` upload zones "on Laguna"** — my probe was dumping every
  `[data-fp-input]` in the page's static markup, including hidden ones. Only `cover` is visible
  for Laguna, identical to Joyride. No bug. (Flagged by the owner; my diagnostic output was
  misleading, not the code.)
