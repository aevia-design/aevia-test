  handle both. Single-cover templates verified byte-identical.
- **`functions/upload.js`** — cover files with a `slotIndex` get a distinct GCS name
  (`cover/cover-<i>.ext`); `photoManifest.cover` becomes a slot-ordered **array** (string for
  single-cover). Mirrors the existing multi-photo special-page pattern.
- **`functions/index.js`** — `signedUrls.cover`/`derivativeUrls.cover` sign each element when the
  manifest is an array; `storedNames.cover` passes the shape through.
- **`pages/staff/template-engine.html`** — deriv/orig cover merge (~4529) + order-ingestion (~4551)
  handle string OR array; the 4 covers land in `specialPhotos.cover` in slot order.
