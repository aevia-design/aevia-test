### 3. EBIT diagnosis (computed in Python; no Excel calc engine available)

## Decisions
- **Price is an OUTPUT of the business case, not an input** — stripped all price assumptions from the
  marketing brief; €70 stays live but is a number to revisit, not a fact.
- **Won't raise price at launch** — can't out-trust CEWE (~€60 incl. delivery) on day one; Aevia is
  already ~25% above at €75 incl. delivery. Revisit once reviews/trust exist.
- **Don't model a conversion lift** — it's a spreadsheet cheat; measure it via a promo test.
- **Consulting kept OUT of aevia's standalone P&L on purpose** — owner wants to see when *aevia alone*
  is viable; combining with consulting indefinitely is explicitly not the goal.
