## Bugs found + fixed (the E2E gate earned its keep)

### 1. Cover render crash — `COVER_BLEED_MM is not defined` (customer-preview)
- Customer preview failed to load: *"Could not load preview — COVER_BLEED_MM is not defined."*
- **Root cause (S28 regression):** S28 extracted cover-caption layout into
  `applyCoverCaptionLayout()` and carried the `COVER_BLEED_MM` const with it, but the
  photo-slot code back in `renderCover()` still referenced it → out of scope. Wander has
  no cover photo slot (`slots:[]`) so the line never ran in S28's smoke test; Scribble's
  cover slot hit it. Fix: re-declared `const COVER_BLEED_MM = 18` inside the `if(slotDef)`
  block ([customer-preview.html:1331]). Parity with staff engine (which already had it).

### 2. False "1 empty photo slot" blocks approve (special-page slots)
- A complete Scribble book couldn't be approved: *"1 empty photo slot."* Diagnosed via
  browser console: the null was at **FP1 (Story page) right slot 0** — a `pool:'special'`
  slot whose photo comes from `specialPhotos`, not the rearrangeable pool.
- **Root cause:** `autoArrange` sets special/artwork slots to `null` in `bookAssignments`
  (photo lives in `specialPhotos`), and `checkBookComplete` counted that `null` as an
  empty slot. Staff didn't warn because its arrange leaves zero-regular-slot sides as `[]`;
  the customer side got a stray `null` in that position via interaction (special slots
  were draggable/droppable — the write-path).
- **Fix (root cause, parity-correct):** added optional `specialSlots` param to
  `checkBookComplete` (`assets/js/book-completeness.js`); both engines record special-slot
  positions during render (`window._specialSlots`, key `"spread:side:slotIdx"`) and pass
  them at the approve ([customer-preview]) + save ([template-engine]) call sites. Special
  nulls are skipped in the empty count. Recording happens in `renderSpread` so it covers
  both fresh AND loaded books. **3 new jest tests; suite 83/83 green.**

## UX batch (this session)

### Story-page photos locked on customer side (decision: ADR 0004)
- Special (FP) photos are provided at order time and shouldn't be customer-editable. Made
  special slots non-draggable/non-droppable on the customer side; dblclick or drop shows a
  toast: *"This photo was chosen for your Story page when you ordered, so it can't be moved
  here. Contact us if you'd like to change it."* (`SPECIAL_LOCK_MSG`). This also closes the
  write-path that produced the stray null in bug #2.
- **Solutioning decision (reversible):** lock now (Option A); defer customer "replace from
  pool / upload new" (Options B/C) until a real customer asks. See `docs/decisions/0004`.

Staff should see photos by the **customer's original filename** (e.g. `IMG_2156`), and the
chronological sort fallback should use those names' numbers. Customer side: **hide names**.
- **Pipeline fact:** `originalName` IS captured at upload (`functions/upload.js:122`) but

  Touches: `upload.js` (manifest), `getOrder` (return originalNames), both engines'
  thumbnail render + the sort, customer thumbnail name-hiding. Needs a **fresh upload** to
  test; old orders fall back to `photo_NNN`.

### #2 — Approve→Pay flow fix (researched, recommended, NOT approved-to-build)
Current post-approve state shows terminal copy *"approved… we'll be in touch soon"* next to
a Pay button → "am I done?" abandonment risk. **Research (Baymard et al.):** keep the
explicit approval (good sign-off for made-to-order), but (a) add a Review→Approve→Pay
progress cue, (b) replace terminal copy with forward copy *"Design approved & locked — one
last step: complete payment to send to print,"* (c) move "we'll be in touch" to the
post-payment state only ([customer-preview.html:705-706]), (d) show price on the Pay button.
All low-effort copy/markup; no backend. Full notes in this session's chat.
