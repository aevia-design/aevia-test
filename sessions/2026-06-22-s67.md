### 11:30 — overlayAbovePhotos z-order (the key new concept)
**Decided:** New per-spread flag `overlayAbovePhotos`. When true, the SVG decorative overlay renders ABOVE the photos (paper-cut shapes layer on top). When false/omitted, overlay stays BEHIND photos (existing default — photos on top).
**Why:** Papercut's aesthetic = cutout shapes overlapping photos. Evgeny: for SP4 and the Cover specifically, photos must stay on top (overlay behind). All other spreads: overlay on top.
**How implemented:** In renderPage (engine + customer-preview), the overlay `<img>` is created but its `canvas.appendChild` is DEFERRED into a `pendingOverlay` var, appended after all slots+captions when the flag is set. Scribble/Newborn untouched (flag absent → behind, as before).
**Watch out for:** PDF (`export-pdf.js`) NOT yet handling this — Stage 5 must verify whether PDF draws overlay-then-photo always (which would be WRONG for the above-photo spreads) or needs per-spread ordering. This is the main open risk.

### — FP1 heart photo hidden behind overlay
**Decided:** `FP1.overlayAbovePhotos: true → false` in papercut-data.js.
**Why:** With `true`, the SVG overlay sits at z-index:2 and fully covers the heart-clipped photo (z-index:1). Papercut's FP Birthday 02 Right.svg is the heart graphic itself (not a transparent-window SVG like Scribble's), so the photo must render on top. Setting `false` drops the overlay to z-index:0; the heart-clipped photo slot at z-index:1 renders above it.
**Watch out for:** FP1 left page has no photo slots, so overlayAbovePhotos direction doesn't matter there — the balloon/cloud decorations stay visible either way.
