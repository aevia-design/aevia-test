### Why the SVG failed and the PSD worked
- The SVG renders photoreal in plain chromium, and the two page layers (`_Image6`/`_Image8`) ARE swappable — BUT Affinity's SVG export **flattened the blend-mode layers**: the top "Highlights"/"Shadows"/"Contrast" layers became opaque copies of the whole original scene (with the example beach photos baked in), hiding any swapped pages. SVG is therefore a dead end for re-skinning.
- The **PSD preserves the real layer stack with blend modes**. Read it with `ag-psd` + `@napi-rs/canvas` (prebuilt, no node-gyp). Structure: artwork = two MULTIPLY layers `"Left page "` (trailing space!) + `"Right page"` over white page bases; `"Highlights"` (screen) sheen; `"Shadows"` (linear-burn) studio shadow; a top `"Contrast"` ADJUSTMENT layer (no extractable pixels). Two HIDDEN 2539² smart-object source layers share the names `"Left page"`/`"Right page"` — must skip hidden layers or the name lookup grabs the wrong one.

- **Reviewer-agent pass** (visual, per Evgeny's request) + an independent LLM critique → applied the two reliable code fixes: (1) final `sharp` grade `.linear(1.07,-4).modulate({brightness:1.05,saturation:1.03})` ≈ the skipped Contrast adjustment → pages now warm/bright not grey (issue #5); (2) `withOpacity()` alpha-scaling helper softens "Shadows" to 0.5 (linear-burn≈multiply was too heavy, issue #4) and "BG Highlights" to 0.6 (top spine bump, issue #7). NOTE: reviewer wrongly assumed `layerRaw('Contrast')` works (no pixels) and that sharp `composite` takes `opacity` (it doesn't) — both corrected in the actual implementation.
