## What shipped (`4c382a8`, pushed → Cloudflare)
Evgeny's three complaints about the optional-spreads selector: (1) redundant "Free"
badge on every card under an "all free" header; (2) descriptions too long, ugly on
mobile; (3) dead space below the fixed-size preview photo (worst on Wander).

**New shared stylesheet `assets/css/addons.css`** — single source of truth; every
product page links it instead of its own drifted inline `.addon*` copy.

- **4 functional templates (Scribble, Papercut, Wander, Newborn)** → vertical
  **photo-card grid** (`.addons-grid`): photo on top (full-width, uniform height),
  name, a **one-line** description, and an `.addon-add` pill that flips
  `Add` → `Added ✓`. Dropped the per-card "Free" badge. Added a `.addons-note`
  helper line under the header ("Add any you'd like; you'll provide their photos
  and details on the next step"). Wander's single card uses `.addons-grid.cols-1`
  (full-width) — directly fixes its dead-space complaint.
- **Removed the photo-count box** ("Your 40-page book will need ~X photos") from
  Scribble/Papercut/Wander — the exact count isn't knowable at this stage.
  `updatePhotoCount()` now early-returns if `#photo-count` is missing, so the
  toggle/chip handlers that still call it are safe no-ops.
- **6 placeholder pages (Sprout, Vows, Devotion, Terrain, Horizon, Radiance)** →
  shared **checkbox-list variant** (`.addons-list`): they have no preview imagery
  and carry priced/"Included" extras (gift wrapping, foil, postcards), so they keep
  the `.chk` checkbox + `.addon-cost` badge. Header stays "Optional add-ons".
- **Selected-card border** rings evenly now: switched the selected state from an
  inset shadow (hidden under the photo at the top, so the bottom looked heavier) to
  an outer `box-shadow: 0 0 0 1px var(--text)`.
- **Rule documented** in `context/style-guide.md`: link `addons.css`, don't restyle
  `.addon*` inline per page; the two variants + their markup.

## Design decision (solutioning, reversible)
Chose vertical photo-card + "Add" pill over keeping a checkbox, and shared CSS +
documented rule over true shared markup (no build step in this project). Whole card
stays clickable to toggle; clicking the photo still opens the lightbox.
