### 2. Newborn AEV-039 — DONE + approved
- Intro caveat handled: Intro is `FPintro` on spread idx 0 (`blankLeft:true`) → renders as
  `[ pure white left | Intro right ]`. It's in the **functional** set, not the 5 content
  spreads. Labour = `FPlabour` idx 11.
- Closed-book fixes Evgeny requested:
  - **Lighting** softened — `Highlights` screen layer → `withOpacity(…,0.5)`; final grade
    `brightness 1.05→1.0`, `saturation 1.03→1.05`. Navy richer, cover photo not blown out.
  - **Warning icons** gone (the `.placement-warn-badge` strip above; needed a re-capture).
- **Spine overhang bug (closed)** — fixed properly via `/systematic-debugging` after two
  wrong guesses. ROOT CAUSE: the spine warps onto a quad calibrated from the `Edge copy`
  shading mask, and **that mask is slightly LARGER than the book** (pokes ~18px above / ~14px
  below the `Book` silhouette). Proven by overlaying `Edge copy` vs `Book` alpha (pure-red =
  outside book). My earlier "taper the quad to the silhouette" attempt was WRONG — it squished
  the caption and made it worse. CORRECT FIX: keep the spine on its true constant-width
  Edge-copy quad (A`[403,746]`/B`[1107,1627]`, EXT`[-4,77]`) **and clip the warped spine to
  the `Book` silhouette alpha** (zero any spine pixel where Book α<128). Removes only the
  overshoot, keeps the spine undistorted.
  - Residual: a ~few-px pale sliver remains at the closed-book top-left corner. It is *inside*
    the Book silhouette (the clip guarantees nothing is outside) — it's the inherent
    disagreement between three independently hand-authored PSD shapes (cover quad / Edge-copy /
    Book) at an acute corner. **Invisible at display size** (renders 2800px, shown ~400–600px).
    Joint decision: **not worth chasing** — ship it.
- **Spine overhang bug (back)** — SAME root cause, more obvious ("spine flying as a rectangle
  outside the book", Evgeny). The `Edge ` spine mask pokes past the `Pages` body at the top of
  the fold. Fixed with the same approach: `clipTo(spine, layerRaw('Pages'))` in
  `compose-back.mjs`. Spine now stops at the book edge.
- **Result:** `mockups/AEV-039/` = 22 PNGs (closed, back, open-00-fpintro … open-19-sp6),
  clean lighting, no badges, flush spine. Approved.

### 3. Wander AEV-040 — BLOCKED (headless photo fetch)
`#262262` chosen for `COVER_HEX` (Wander = cream front `#f2ede3`, navy back+spine `#262262`).
Capture fails: order load never completes → `#order-info-panel` stays hidden → 180s timeout.

- ROOT CAUSE (confirmed): `loadOrderIntoEngine` downloads photos with **`Promise.all`**
  (`template-engine.html` ~4194 special, ~4229 pool). `urlToFile` throws on a failed fetch, so
  **ONE failed photo rejects the whole load** → `showOrderInfoPanel`/`renderBook` never run.
- The failing photos are **RANDOM each run** (030/037 → 013/034) with `net::ERR_FAILED` from
  GCS — i.e. NOT corrupt files and NOT auth/CORS (those would fail all 37). It's headless
  Chromium dropping ~2 of the ~37 **heavy parallel** fetches under resource pressure. Renders
  fine in Evgeny's real browser (more headroom).
- Tried + did NOT fix it: timeouts 60s→180s; fetch **retry** (5×, backoff); fetch
  **concurrency throttle to 6**. Still ~2 random drops. Added `requestfailed` logging (that's
  how we got the URLs).
