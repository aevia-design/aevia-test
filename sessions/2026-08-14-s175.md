# Session 175 — 2026-08-14

The caption AI button had one behaviour and three different jobs to do. Scoped it to where AI
belongs, built a compose mode for Our story, caught it inventing facts about a couple's wedding,
and found the cause was a sentence I had written myself.

---

### The problem, as the owner framed it

The ✦ Generate button was attached to **every** caption-allowed slot and **every** text panel,
with no data flag gating it. Fine on a standard spread. Wrong on a special page, where the text
is the customer's own — a birthday message, vows, why-I-love-him, the birth story — collected
on the order form. The request body was `{ image, collection, previousCaptions }`: the
customer's text was never sent, so clicking Generate on Heirloom's "Why I love him" discarded
their words and substituted a caption invented from the facing photograph.

Owner's framing was that some pages need *generation*, some need *enhancement of what the
customer wrote*, and some need no button at all. Building the per-template inventory first is
what made the rest decidable.

### The inventory that drove every decision

Built from the `orderFormMeta` blocks in each `*-data.js`. Kept in
`docs/briefs/caption-ai-modes.md`; **do not re-derive it.**

**Owner's calls, all settled:**
- Standard spreads keep the button unchanged. "It works and it works well."
- All 19 functional page definitions (28 instances with Heirloom's colourways) lose it.
- **Tender FPwords and Heirloom FPhim/FPher lose it too** — vows and why-you-love-your-spouse.
  "Customer would enter it themselves probably with use of AI already, don't want to re-write."
  This collapsed a proposed three-mode design down to one.
- Our story (Tender + Heirloom ×4) gets compose: weld the two order-form answers into prose.
- **No AI on the travel-map itinerary.** It is a formatting job.

### 2026-08-14 — It invented a wedding

**Done:** `c44d0f9`. Found in post-deploy smoke testing, fixed same session.

Given *"We talked all night and missed the last train home, so we walked"*, compose returned
*"...enjoying each other's company **under the stars**, laying the foundation for a relationship
that would soon **blossom**."* Stars invented, feeling invented, `blossom` is the greeting-card
register the voice file bans two sections earlier. A second case turned `"coffee"` into
`"coffee dates"`.

**The dominant cause was a sentence I wrote: "45–65 words."** On an editing task a floor is an
instruction to invent — given 21 words of source, the missing 24 can only come from the model.
Three lesser causes: no `temperature` set (creative default), the no-invention rule living only
in a system prompt that is otherwise 60 lines of creative-caption coaching, and nothing saying
that doing very little was acceptable.

**Fix:** ceiling only (never >65, no minimum, no target), `temperature: 0.2`, rules repeated in
the user message as a numbered list, and "returning their words nearly unchanged is a success"
stated in both places. Both real failures are now worked negative examples in the voice file.
**Verified on the exact failing inputs:** Graz 21w → 21w (only `friends` → `friend's`), coffee
18w → 17w, and a 5-word input → 7w. Grammar and joining only.
**Watch out for:** **never reintroduce a length floor here.** The brief says so explicitly; it
is the change most likely to get "tidied" back in by someone who thinks a range looks precise.
