### 2026-08-13 — #112's breaker was hiding the photos it never tried
**Done:** `7864391`. The consecutive-failure breaker stops the queue by design, so slots
behind it are **never attempted** — they fail nothing, so nothing recorded them.
`uploadFailures` under-reported the damage: AEV-096 named slot 29 and said nothing about
30–56. The customer was told one photo was missing when 28 were, and the dashboard agreed.
**Decided:** Fixed by DERIVING the missing set, not tracking it. Every worker claims with
`nextIndex++`, so after `Promise.all` any index ≥ final `nextIndex` was never claimed.
~25 lines, no new state. Codex proposed positive `succeeded`-set tracking (~40 lines); the
derivation gives the same invariant more cheaply because `uploadFailures` was verified
complete for every ATTEMPTED slot (one push at `:2836`, `attempt === maxRetries`).
**Why it matters:** `uploadFailures` now means exactly **"not in GCS"** — the invariant any
future Retry must trust. A Retry re-sending only recorded failures would have confirmed
books with photos missing.
**Watch out for:** **the existing `#112` qa case could never have caught this.** It scatters
its five failures, so a success always resets the counter and the breaker never fires — that
path had ZERO coverage. New case added (`qa:order` now 19/19) and **confirmed red first**:
without the fix it reports "47 missing slots unreported".

### 2026-08-14 — About-page photos: six rejected layouts, then the owner's own idea
**Done:** `17135f0` + `756d771`. The `img-break` placeholders are gone; four real photos now
run as a **rolling row**. EN + DE, copy masters updated.
**Why the grids all failed:** these are **wide environmental shots** — the subjects sit small
in the frame. Any layout placing four side by side shrinks the faces to nothing, worst on a
phone. A row can run them large and does not care how narrow the screen is.
**Decided (owner):** the row, Option C caption — names left, venue credit right.
Caption: *"Founders of Aevia: Eugene and Xenia. Our studio, most days."*
**"Eugene" is deliberate** — the owner's public spelling of Evgenii for the Austrian
audience, who misspell it. **First time either founder is named on the site**; this sets the
convention. Recorded in `docs/website-copy-EN.md`.
**The cropping rule, learned the hard way:** an early attempt cut the owner's legs to force a
square frame and was rejected. The rule is **trim dead space freely, never cut a person** —
which is NOT "no crops". Under it, all four photos matched to one ratio (0.835, the desk
photo's native) by removing only empty ceiling: 581px off the two wide shots, 410px off the
standing portrait. Heads and feet intact at every breakpoint.
**Watch out for:** **`var(--muted)` does not pass AA on `--surface`** — 3.94:1 at full
opacity, and the 0.72 opacity this shipped with made it 2.51:1. `design-principles.md` only
warns it is borderline on `--bg`. The venue credit is **contractually required** for the
photoshoot location, so near-invisible was a business risk as well as an a11y failure. Now
`#6f6660`, 4.90:1.
