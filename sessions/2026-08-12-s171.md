### 11:30 — The white line on AEV-095's printed cover is bleed, and is fine

**Done:** Measured the cover SVG against the PDF's bleed expansion. The navy back rect
falls 0.19mm short at the bottom, Clémence's painting 0.49mm. Both gaps sit **17.5–17.8mm
outside the trim**, in the last half-millimetre of an 18mm bleed. No change made.

**Why:** Two upstream Illustrator artefacts — the artwork is anchored ~0.19mm high
(y ≈ −51.56 where the bleed edge is −51.024), and the raster's transform was written as
`scale(.177)` where 0.177212 is needed to fill its 236mm box. **Not** caused by the 300 DPI
re-encode; pixel dimensions were preserved, so the placement maths is unchanged.

**Watch out for:** Do not hand-patch the SVG — a re-export undoes it (S157). And
`tests/cover-svg-viewbox.test.js` **cannot see this class of defect**: it checks the
viewBox frames the trim, not that artwork fills the bleed. Harmless at 0.5mm, would print
at 5mm. Logged as TO-DOS #109.
