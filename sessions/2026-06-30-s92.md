## Decisions made

- **Reassure, don't build a magnifier.** Photo-quality worry is a communication gap, not a quality defect (print uses originals). Cheap copy + the existing upload warning is the industry-standard fix. Hold a true full-res "inspect" feature unless the trial shows copy isn't enough; if built later, use a ~2000–3000px mid-res derivative (art-storefront pattern), never the original, to bound egress.
- **Keep optimized in BOTH engines.** Owner's intuition was "full quality in customer engine" — research says leaders deliberately don't, and for Aevia it costs egress + risks backfire. Rejected.
- **Help delivery copy → Austria-only** (matches the Phase-2 checkout), not "across the EU".
- **Ordering flow facts:** 24h turnaround, single preview (NOT "two layout options"), change-request loop before pay.
