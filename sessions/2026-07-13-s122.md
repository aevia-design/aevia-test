### 08:42 — GCS upload aborts are benign
**Decided:** the 15 `net::ERR_ABORTED` PUTs to `storage.googleapis.com` are **not** a defect — no finding raised.
**Why:** `order.html:2255` wraps each PUT in a 3-attempt retry with backoff, sets `success` only when `putRes.ok`, and throws if all retries fail. No error surfaced and the success screen rendered, so every file landed. The aborts are transient failures the hardening absorbed.
**Watch out for:** 15 aborts out of 54 uploads is a high transient rate on a fast Codespace link. On a slow customer connection this could plausibly exhaust the 3 retries. Worth a deliberate P2 test on a throttled network before launch.
