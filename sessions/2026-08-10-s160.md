### 10:15 — The new colourway drops: I measured the wrong element and reported it
**Decided:** the photo openings did NOT move; my first report that they had was wrong.
**Why:** the covers are `<g data-name="Image"><g clip-path="url(#n)"><rect .../>`. The rect
is a swatch painted inside the clip — its size and position are arbitrary, which is why
they looked wild and non-square (247×274, 262×234). **The opening is the clipPath**, and it
is character-identical in 11 of 12 covers: 226.772 units square (80mm), r 5.674 (2mm), at
x 771.028 y 113.464. The only outlier is *Beige*'s Roots, 1.063 units left — exactly the
`coverFrame`/`coverFrameShifted` split Beige already models.
**Watch out for:** the new colourways therefore need ONE clip variant, not two — less work
than Beige, not more. Read the clip before measuring anything else in a cover SVG.

### 14:30 — Two caption-editing fixes from the owner's eyeball (both engines)
**Done:** (1) the focus wash now flips by ink luminance — light ink gets a dark wash;
(2) the four monogram initials show no placeholder when empty and reveal a dashed outline
on hover.
**Why:** (1) `.slot-caption:focus` set a fixed `rgba(255,255,255,0.75)`. Fine under Beige's
dark ink, but Brown and Green put LIGHT ink on a dark cover — the text vanished while being
typed and returned on blur, when the wash went. Flipping the wash rather than the ink keeps
what staff type identical to what prints, and luminance means any future colourway is
handled with no per-template rule. Beige and Blue are unchanged, verified.
(2) TWO placeholders were leaking into 8×9mm boxes: `.slot-caption:empty::before` →
'Add caption…' on the intro letters, and `.cover-caption:empty::before` → `attr(data-label)`
= 'Monogram — first initial' on the back cover. Either wraps one character per line. This
also closes the S158 open question about those boxes having no empty-state hint.
**Watch out for:** the hover outline reaches the CUSTOMER preview too (shared class). If it
reads as fussy there, restrict it to the staff engine.
