### 10:20 — Mockups need 4 orders, not 12 — and I wired the wrong script first
**Decided:** ONE order per colourway; the three monograms come from that same order.
**Why:** switching the TEMPLATE in the engine resets `specialPhotos`
(template-engine.html:4554), so a colourway cannot be swapped inside an order. Switching the
MONOGRAM does not — it moves artwork and letter positions only (the S158 design), so photos
and captions survive. New `qa/select-monogram.mjs` drives the picker headlessly; it throws
rather than falling back, because silently capturing the default monogram under another
monogram's filename would poison a whole set.
**Watch out for:** I first wired `web-mockups.mjs`. **That is the OLD image path** — the live
product pages read `assets/images/mockups/exp2/<template>/`, produced by `exp2-images.mjs`
(+ `compose-flat-mockup.mjs` for front-new/back-new). Reverted and rewired. Heirloom spreads
resolve by book-sequence **id**, not by `open-NN-` index, because the index shifts with photo
count; proved with a fixture using deliberately shifted indices.

### 11:40 — Letter geometry: the same CSV slip twice, so it now has a checker
**Done:** all four colourways synced; `scripts/check-heirloom-letters.mjs` added

**Why:** the owner nudged the letters, but twice a nudge went into the without-bleed cell and

### 13:10 — Order text never reached the page: renderBook resets bookCaptions
**Done:** root cause found and fixed; `qa/verify-order-text-seeding.mjs` guards it.
**Why:** `loadOrderIntoEngine` seeds FP text panels (step 7) and cover captions (step 8), then
step 9 called `renderBook()` again to paint the monogram initials. **`renderBook` resets
`window.bookCaptions` on a full rebuild**, discarding everything just seeded — including the
initials it had itself written one line earlier. Heirloom-only: step 9 re-renders only when
`applyMonogramInitials` returns true, and no other template has monogram letters, which is
exactly why the owner remembered Tender working. `renderBook` now takes
`{ preserveCaptions: true }`, used by that one call site.
**Watch out for:** a comment at line 5060 claimed renderBook does NOT clear bookCaptions while
another at 5181 said it does. The second was right; the first cost me twenty minutes. Both
now correct. **Orders SAVED while blank keep their blanks** — the `restoring` branch returns
before seeding, by design, to protect customer edits. Those orders need re-placing.

### 17:10 — The monogram picker is hidden in order mode; the script does not care
**Decided (owner):** staff do NOT need to change a monogram in the engine — the customer's
product-page choice stands. No engine change; do not re-raise.
**Why it came up:** the owner tried and could not. `setMode` sets `#special-panel` to

the 4-order plan stands, but **the pre-capture eyeball I recommended is impossible**; the
preview is the captured `cover-wrap-<order>-<mono>.png`, checked before composing.
