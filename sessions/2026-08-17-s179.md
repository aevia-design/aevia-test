### 2026-08-17 — Sweeping the registry for a design skill, and finding none

40 queries against `skills.sh`, then **read the candidate `SKILL.md` files in full** rather than
scoring from names (the S120 method rule). Verdict, which the owner accepted:

**No skill on the registry designs a packaging layout.** The category splits three ways and the
middle is empty:
- **Briefing** (`arnabbagxd/brand-building-skills/brand-packaging`, 763 installs) — interviews
  you, outputs a brief *for a designer you are not hiring*. Ends exactly where the owner is stuck.
- **Generation** (`eachlabs/skills/packaging-design-generation`) — a paid image-gen API wrapper.
  Makes pictures, not vector artwork.
- **Critique** (`anthropics/knowledge-work-plugins/design-critique`, 3.8k) — downstream judging.

Two genuinely useful finds worth remembering:
- **`sebastian-software/effective-print-design`** (302) — real print typography discipline
  (absolute units, minimum weights, "thin vanishes", bleed, per-locale dashes). Aimed at
  HTML/CSS-to-paper, which is exactly how the studies were built.
- **`sfkislev/flue`** → `illustrator` — a working shell-to-Illustrator ExtendScript bridge.
  **Not installed and not needed** — the owner does not have Illustrator locally, only Xenia does,
  and the studies carry millimetre coordinates she can place by hand.

**Owner challenged whether the skills were being USED or merely named.** Fair, and worth
answering concretely next time too: the mm/pt specs and the "where this breaks" notes come from
`effective-print-design`; the pre-design questions came from `brand-packaging`'s gather list; its
"packaging is applied brand identity" rule is why the palette and tagline were pulled from
`context/style-guide.md` rather than invented. **Neither skill supplied the layouts** — no skill
does, which was the finding.

**Decided: skills were read, not installed.** Installing puts them in `~/.claude/skills`
permanently where they auto-load by description on unrelated work. Offered; owner did not take it
up ("i don't think it would become recurring").

### 2026-08-17 — Five studies, at true scale

Built as one page, five envelopes: **A Quiet Mark · B Full Measure · C The Letter · D The Mark ·
E Ledger**. The variable across them is **how much the envelope says**, from a lone 44 mm glyph to
a four-line letter — not five shuffles of the same parts. Saved to `work/packaging/` and published
privately at <https://claude.ai/code/artifact/cbeddb52-a9b6-4d3b-83fc-5c12280663b6>.

**Owner's correction mid-session, which improved it:** small text should be **printed, not
debossed**, and the supplier prints any colour. That became the page's governing rule — **deboss
carries the large forms, print carries the small text** — and removed the legibility risk from
studies B, C and E. It is also what the references do: reference 1's flap paragraph is printed.

**Watch out for:** `filter` is applied **before** `mask` in CSS. A `drop-shadow` on the same
element that carries `mask-image` is computed on the unmasked box and then clipped to nothing —
the debossed lockup rendered *invisible* in Study A. Fix is to split them: `filter` on the parent,
`mask` on the child. Caught only because the page was screenshotted before publishing.

**Watch out for:** the artifact viewer's CSP blocks external fonts, so the page falls back to
Georgia. Lora is what the real artwork uses. Judge placement and scale from it, not typeface.
