## Key conclusions
1. **Pricing → €89–99, leaning €95 shipping-included** (up from €66–69). Aevia is a done-for-you
   gift/occasion object, NOT "premium CEWE" — €66–69 is the worst square (loses the commodity
   shopper, doesn't signal premium, invites the CEWE comparison it loses). Variable cost ≈
   €15–16/book → 77–84% margin at any price considered; pricing is a demand/positioning question,
   not cost-plus. The friends' price-pushback experiment was flawed (allowed direct CEWE compare).
2. **Marketing → model bottom-up from CAC, kill the flat 10%-of-revenue line.** Paid = `budget÷CAC`
   (linear); organic = ramping assumption (capped); referral = % of total orders.
   - Paid-only premium photobook ≈ **1.6:1** LTV:CAC (avoid); **organic-led ≈ 3:1+**.
   - Artifact Uprising (closest analog) grew organic (IG/Pinterest/UGC/$10-$10 referral), not paid.
   - **Make-or-break = can we build the organic engine to keep blended CAC ~€30.**
3. **CAC benchmarks are US/global-skewed (€45–70 paid), NOT Austrian** — Austrian ad costs likely
   lower; pull DACH-specific data before locking the model.
4. **Content ops** = build-it-yourself with Xenia (marketing generalist) + AI, no agency yr 0–1.
   AI multiplies/polishes REAL assets, never fabricates fake scenes (=slop). Starter stack
   ~€30–70/mo (Canva Pro + Midjourney + CapCut + Meta Business Suite; Firefly for legal-safe;
   avoid Sora, discontinued). First-months cadence M0 setup → M3 small paid boost to MEASURE real CAC.
