# Aevia — Tone of Voice

The single reference for how Aevia writes to customers. If a page reads differently
from this, the page is wrong. (Supersedes the short "Copy Tone" note in
`design-principles.md`, which now points here.)

---

## The voice in one line

Warm, precise, European. A calm concierge who has done this many times and wants you
to feel looked after — not a brand shouting about itself.

---

## Principles

1. **Warm within restraint.** Personal and human, never gushing. The reassurance comes
   from precision, not adjectives. "You approve before we print" does more than "amazing".
2. **Short sentences.** One idea each. If a sentence needs a comma to breathe twice,
   split it.
3. **Speak to "you".** Address the customer directly. Aevia is "we". Avoid "users",
   "customers", "one".
4. **Active voice.** A person does the thing. "We print from your originals", not
   "your book is printed from the originals".
5. **No hype, no exclamation marks.** The product is the proof.
6. **Plain over technical.** Explain in kitchen-table language. Drop jargon
   ("industry-standard payment processor") unless a customer would actually say it.
7. **Honest about early access.** We don't overpromise. If something is still being
   finalised, we say so and say when.

---

## Word list

**Prefer:** considered · precise · quietly · calm · looked after · built to last ·
good light · we'll tell you · you approve before you pay

**Avoid:** amazing · stunning · world-class · game-changing · effortless · seamless ·
unleash · elevate · curated (overused) · "best-in-class" · any exclamation mark

---

## Mechanical rules (the stop-slop pass)

Every customer-facing line gets a `/stop-slop` pass before it ships. Specifically:

- **No em dashes.** Use a full stop, a comma, or a colon.
- **No filler openers** ("Here's the thing", "It's worth noting").
- **No lazy extremes** ("always", "never", "everyone") unless literally true.
- **No three-in-a-row** lists or three sentences of identical length. Vary the rhythm.
- **Cut adverbs** that add nothing ("simply", "easily", "quickly" as padding).

## Before / after

**Payment**
- ✗ We accept all major credit and debit cards via Stripe — a secure, industry-standard
  payment processor. You are only charged after you approve your book layout, not upfront.
- ✓ We take all major credit and debit cards through Stripe, which handles payment
  securely. You're only charged once you've approved your book, never before.

**Preview quality**
- ✗ This preview is optimized to load quickly — your book is printed from your original,
  full-resolution photos.
- ✓ This preview is optimized to load quickly, then we print your book from your original,
  full-resolution photos.

**Photo count**
- ✗ Depending on the template, you'll need between 50 and 80 photos.
- ✓ It depends on the template. Send what you have, and we'll tell you exactly how many
  we'll use as we assemble your book.

## When in doubt

Read it aloud. If it sounds like a person reassuring a friend who is about to spend
real money on something they care about, it's right. If it sounds like a brand, cut it back.
